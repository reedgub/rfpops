# Key Personnel — Stratus Federal Solutions

---

## Dr. Patricia Ashworth
**Title:** Chief Technology Officer
**Clearance:** Secret (Active)

Dr. Ashworth brings 22 years of federal IT experience to Stratus, including a decade as a contractor supporting NIST's Information Technology Laboratory. Her work at NIST included contributions to NIST Special Publication development and federal cloud security framework guidance. She holds an AWS Certified Solutions Architect — Professional certification and a CISSP, and she holds a doctorate in Information Systems from George Mason University. Dr. Ashworth serves as the firm's technical authority on FedRAMP, NIST 800-53 compliance, and cloud security architecture decisions across all active engagements.

## Marcus Delgado
**Title:** Cloud Practice Lead
**Clearance:** Top Secret (Active)

Marcus leads Stratus's cloud infrastructure practice with 14 years of experience in federal cloud architecture and migration. He is an AWS GovCloud specialist with deep familiarity with GovCloud networking, IAM boundary conditions, and the operational differences between GovCloud and commercial AWS environments. Marcus is the firm's primary FedRAMP subject matter expert and has led or directly supported eight successful FedRAMP authorizations across Moderate and High impact levels. He holds multiple AWS certifications including Solutions Architect Professional and Security Specialty.

## Jennifer Kowalski
**Title:** Security and Compliance Lead
**Clearance:** Top Secret (Active)

Jennifer leads Stratus's security and compliance practice, bringing more than 15 years of federal information security experience. She spent six years as a DHS contractor supporting security assessment and authorization activities across multiple DHS components before joining Stratus. Jennifer holds CISSP and CISM certifications and has prior experience as a 3PAO assessor, giving her a detailed understanding of how authorizations are evaluated from the assessor's perspective. She is also a CMMC Registered Practitioner and leads all CMMC-related client engagements at Stratus.
