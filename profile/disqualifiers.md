# Hard No-Bid Criteria — Stratus Federal Solutions

The following conditions trigger an automatic no-bid regardless of other opportunity characteristics. These are not judgment calls — they reflect firm constraints on capacity, certification, and operational viability.

- Contracts valued under $250,000. Below this threshold, the contract cannot support Stratus's overhead structure and produces negative margin after direct labor and indirect costs.

- Requirements for Top Secret/SCI cleared personnel. Stratus holds a Secret facility clearance with three personnel at Top Secret, but the firm does not hold SCI accreditation and cannot staff SCI-billet positions.

- Health information exchange (HIE) work for non-federal health systems. Stratus has no domain expertise in state and local HIE interoperability standards (e.g., HL7 FHIR implementation at the state HIE layer, CommonWell, Carequality). Federal health IT work such as CMS-overseen MMIS systems is within scope; standalone state HIE platform work is not.

- Any RFP requiring more than 50 FTE on contract within 90 days of award. Stratus cannot surge to 50+ FTE within a 90-day mobilization window without compromising quality or cannibalizing existing program staffing.

- Prime contractor roles on contracts exceeding $15 million total value. Stratus's bonding capacity ceiling does not support prime responsibility on contracts above this threshold. Subcontractor roles on larger contracts remain viable.

- Oracle database administration as a core deliverable. Stratus has no Oracle-certified staff on bench and does not carry Oracle licensing or partnership credentials. Engagements where Oracle DBA work is incidental or managed by the government may be evaluated case by case.

- CONUS-only work requiring 100% on-site presence outside the Washington DC metro area for more than six months. Stratus is headquartered in Tysons Corner and the majority of staff are locally based. Extended full-time relocations create unacceptable retention risk and logistics overhead.
