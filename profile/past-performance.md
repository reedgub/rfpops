## Entry 1: VA Cloud Migration

| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| **Agency** | Department of Veterans Affairs, Office of Information & Technology |
| **Period of Performance** | 2023–2024 |
| **Contract Value** | $2,100,000 |
| **Duration** | 14 months |
| **Role** | Prime Contractor |

**Scope of Work**

Stratus served as prime contractor for the migration of 47 legacy applications from on-premises VA data centers to AWS GovCloud. The engagement began with a comprehensive application discovery and dependency assessment phase, followed by migration wave planning, data migration execution, and cutover coordination with VA's Office of Information and Technology operations teams. Work included updating FedRAMP authorization packages for affected systems, coordinating with the VA's Authorizing Official, and managing a 90-day hypercare period post-cutover to stabilize migrated workloads and resolve integration issues.

**Outcomes**

Stratus completed the engagement six weeks ahead of the contracted schedule. System uptime of 99.97% was maintained across all migration windows, with zero unplanned outages impacting VA service delivery. Twelve applications achieved FedRAMP High authorization as a result of the security uplift work performed during migration.

**Relevance**

This engagement directly demonstrates Stratus's ability to lead large-scale federal cloud migration programs, manage FedRAMP authorization activities across a large application portfolio, and deliver results within a major VA organizational context. The engagement is the firm's largest completed federal prime contract to date.

## Entry 2: DHS FedRAMP Authorization Support

| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| **Agency** | Department of Homeland Security, Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA) |
| **Period of Performance** | 2022–2023 |
| **Contract Value** | $890,000 |
| **Duration** | 8 months |
| **Role** | Prime Contractor |

**Scope of Work**

Stratus provided full FedRAMP Moderate authorization support for a CISA cloud-based threat intelligence platform that had not previously achieved authorization. The engagement covered the complete authorization documentation package: System Security Plan (SSP), Security Assessment Plan (SAP), Security Assessment Report (SAR), and Plan of Action and Milestones (POA&M). Stratus coordinated the engagement with a designated 3PAO throughout the assessment cycle, managing information requests, evidence collection, and control validation activities. Work also included remediation advisory support for findings surfaced during the 3PAO assessment.

**Outcomes**

CISA received the authorization letter 11 weeks after conclusion of the 3PAO assessment. The assessment produced zero critical findings — a result attributable to the pre-assessment gap remediation Stratus completed before the formal review began. Notably, this was the first successful FedRAMP authorization for this platform after two prior authorization attempts by other vendors had failed.

**Relevance**

This engagement provides direct evidence of Stratus's depth in FedRAMP authorization process management, 3PAO coordination, and complex federal security documentation. It also demonstrates the firm's ability to succeed where prior efforts by other vendors had not.

Stratus served as a named subcontractor under Booz Allen Hamilton for a DevSecOps modernization effort supporting mission-critical Air Force logistics software managed by AFLCMC. This engagement demonstrates Stratus's Air Force program familiarity, its DevSecOps pipeline technical depth, and its OSCAL/compliance-as-code implementation capability. It also shows the firm's track record of delivering value effectively within large prime contractor program structures.

## Entry 4: State Medicaid System Migration

| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| **Agency** | State of Maryland, Department of Health |
| **Period of Performance** | 2021–2023 |
| **Contract Value** | $3,200,000 |
| **Duration** | 22 months |
| **Role** | Prime Contractor |

**Scope of Work**

Stratus served as prime contractor for the migration of Maryland's Medicaid Management Information System (MMIS) from a legacy Oracle-based on-premises infrastructure to a cloud-native architecture hosted on Azure Government. The engagement included application re-architecture, data migration across 2.3 million beneficiary records, integration with Maryland's existing health IT systems, and CMS certification support. Stratus coordinated directly with CMS reviewers throughout the certification process and prepared all required technical documentation for the federal review.

**Outcomes**

The MMIS received CMS certification on first submission, avoiding the multi-round review cycles that commonly affect MMIS certification efforts. The new cloud-native architecture delivered a projected $1.8 million annual reduction in infrastructure operating costs compared to the legacy system. The migration was completed with zero data loss across all 2.3 million beneficiary records.

**Relevance**

This engagement demonstrates Stratus's ability to manage a large, complex data migration with federal oversight (CMS), deliver on Azure Government, and support certification processes governed by federal standards. It also reflects the firm's largest completed prime contract by value and its ability to execute multi-year engagements successfully.
