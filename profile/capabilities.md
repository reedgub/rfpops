# Capabilities Statement — Stratus Federal Solutions

**Stratus Federal Solutions** is a 28-person IT services firm headquartered in Tysons Corner, Virginia. Founded to serve the federal government's most demanding cloud and cybersecurity requirements, Stratus generates approximately $6.2M in annual revenue, with 80% derived from federal civilian and defense agency contracts.

---

## Core Services

**Cloud Migration and Modernization**
Stratus plans and executes cloud migrations for federal agencies moving workloads from legacy on-premises infrastructure to AWS GovCloud and Azure Government. Our migration practice covers application assessment, dependency mapping, phased cutover planning, and post-migration hypercare. We have completed migrations ranging from small departmental systems to large, multi-application portfolios across multiple agencies.

**FedRAMP Compliance and Authorization Support**
We provide end-to-end FedRAMP authorization support for cloud service providers and federal agencies. Our team prepares System Security Plans (SSPs), Security Assessment Plans (SAPs), Security Assessment Reports (SARs), and Plans of Action and Milestones (POA&Ms). We coordinate directly with Third-Party Assessment Organizations (3PAOs) and agency Authorizing Officials throughout the authorization process.

**DevSecOps and CI/CD Pipeline Implementation**
Stratus builds and modernizes software delivery pipelines for federal programs, integrating security controls at every stage. Our DevSecOps practice includes GitOps workflow design, container security scanning, SAST/DAST integration, STIG compliance automation, and compliance-as-code using the OSCAL framework.

**Infrastructure Modernization**
We assess and modernize aging federal IT infrastructure, replacing end-of-life systems with cloud-native and containerized architectures. Engagements typically include infrastructure-as-code implementation using Terraform, Kubernetes orchestration, and integration with federal logging and monitoring requirements.

**Cloud Security**
Our security practice covers continuous monitoring, vulnerability management, SIEM implementation using Splunk, and scanning with Nessus. We support agencies in maintaining ongoing authorization to operate (ATO) and responding to audit findings.
